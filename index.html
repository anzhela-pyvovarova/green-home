Care Tips

🌞 Light

Most houseplants prefer bright indirect light

💧 Water

Water only when the soil has dried slightly

🌱 Fertilizer

Feed plants every 2–4 weeks during the growing season

🌡️ Temperature

Most plants grow well at 18–25°C
